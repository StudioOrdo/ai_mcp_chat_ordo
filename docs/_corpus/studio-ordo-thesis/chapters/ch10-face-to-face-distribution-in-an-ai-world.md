# 10. Face-to-Face Distribution in an AI World

As AI floods the internet with cheap content, generic marketing becomes easier to produce and harder to trust.

The scarce thing becomes real human credibility: a recommendation from someone you met, someone you trust, someone in your community, someone who handed you a card and said, “You should talk to this person.”

Studio Ordo is built to amplify that kind of trust.

The workflow is concrete:

1. A business owner receives a unique QR code through Ordo.
2. The QR code can go on a business card, flyer, storefront sign, social profile, event handout, or affiliate card.
3. Studio Ordo can optionally print and ship the physical cards.
4. Affiliates, partners, apprentices, students, or community members can receive their own QR-coded cards.
5. A visitor scans the code and enters a persistent AI conversation.
6. Ordo attributes the lead to the correct source.
7. If the lead books, buys, subscribes, or becomes a customer, the affiliate can be compensated.

The relationship is still human.

The trust is still face-to-face.

The attribution is handled by the system.

The follow-up is handled by the AI.

The compensation becomes visible and fair.

This is not spammy affiliate marketing.

This is a physical-world bridge into AI-operated relationship infrastructure.

> **Ordo turns face-to-face trust into trackable, compensable business infrastructure.**

Because Ordo is designed to reduce onboarding, support, content production, follow-up, and administrative labor, more of the margin can potentially be shared with people who create trusted relationships.

That enables a different model:

> **Use AI to reduce operational drag, then use the savings to reward human trust-building.**

Software should not replace human trust.

Software should make human trust economically visible.
