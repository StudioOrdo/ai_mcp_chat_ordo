# 7. What Ordo Is Not

Studio Ordo is not simply:

* a website builder
* a CRM
* a chatbot
* a course platform
* a media generator
* an affiliate tool
* a low-code platform

It may contain pieces of all of these, but the actual category is different:

> **A conversational AI operating layer for expertise, relationships, and small-business workflows.**

Ordo is not trying to replace Squarespace, Salesforce, Kajabi, Canva, Mailchimp, or ChatGPT one-to-one.

It is trying to make a category that those tools separately approximate but none fully occupies: an AI-native system that understands the whole business and can operate across its digital surfaces from a single governed workspace.
