# 14. Customer-Funded Development

Studio Ordo can support a new model for software development.

Customers do not have to wait for a distant SaaS roadmap.

If they need a specific workflow, tool, vertical pack, report generator, intake system, media pipeline, CRM behavior, or content engine, they can fund its development directly through production credits, tool sponsorship, or workflow commissioning.

A yoga studio might sponsor a class-pack and membership workflow.

A teacher might sponsor a lecture-to-study-guide pipeline.

A consultant might sponsor a client-intake and report-generation system.

A creator might sponsor a transcript-to-subscription-content engine.

These capabilities can be built as MCP tools, workflow packs, or business-specific automations.

Some may remain private to the customer. Some may become shared premium tools. Some may be contributed back to the open-source core. Some may become vertical packs available to other businesses.

Because AI-assisted production lowers development cost, relatively small sponsorships can produce useful software.

The customer funds what they need.

The platform gains new capabilities.

The ecosystem becomes more valuable for everyone.

> **Traditional SaaS roadmaps are centralized. Ordo’s roadmap can be customer-funded, vertical by vertical, need by need.**
