# 6. The Product

Studio Ordo is a conversational AI workspace for experts, educators, creators, consultants, coaches, local experts, and small service businesses.

It combines website, CRM, publishing, media production, search, lead capture, referral tracking, workflow automation, content generation, business intelligence, and AI assistance into one governed system.

The customer does not start by configuring a dashboard.

They start with a conversation.

Ordo asks:

What is your business?

Who do you serve?

What do you offer?

What do people ask you?

What content do you already have?

What do you want to sell?

What should happen when someone contacts you?

What should your business sound like?

Who refers customers to you?

What do you want Ordo to help you do?

Each answer updates the system.

The result is not just a website. It is a working business presence with an AI front desk, content engine, customer memory, media pipeline, referral system, knowledge base, and tool-calling workspace.

The base platform is common.

The lived product becomes bespoke.
