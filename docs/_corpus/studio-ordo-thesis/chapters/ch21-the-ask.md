# 21. The Ask

Studio Ordo is designed to grow without the traditional burden of venture-backed SaaS.

The goal is not to raise money, hire a large team, and chase generic scale.

The goal is to build a trusted, open, managed AI workspace that serves real operators, one use case at a time.

What is useful now:

* introductions to experts, teachers, coaches, consultants, creators, and local service businesses who have knowledge to monetize
* early users who want to turn expertise into content, subscriptions, referrals, or services
* affiliates and community partners willing to test QR-card-based referral flows
* feedback from people who understand small-business operations, education, or open-source platforms
* vertical use cases worth turning into MCP tool packs
* customer-funded development opportunities for specific workflows

What is not needed first:

* a large staff
* venture overhead
* a bloated enterprise stack
* a generic marketing funnel before the product has real relationship-driven proof

Studio Ordo should begin where trust already exists: people, communities, teachers, local experts, and small businesses with something real to offer.
