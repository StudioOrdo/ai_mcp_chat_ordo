# Lightweight paid outreach

A small test budget to see which channel earns trust fastest. This
guide only makes sense after the friends-and-family and local-flyers
presets have already produced at least a handful of introductions.

## Why this is the third preset, not the first

Paid outreach is the easiest preset to run badly. It is expensive to
learn from and easy to mistake motion for progress. The warm and
local presets are cheaper teachers: by the time you arrive here, you
already know which part of your offer is clear, which part stalls,
and which kind of person bothers to reply. Paid channels only
amplify what is already working.

If you are tempted to skip ahead, the most honest thing to do is
pause for a week, run the warm preset, and come back.

## How to run it

1. Pick one paid channel you already understand as a user — the
   channel you have seen ads on, not the one a thread told you to
   try. Familiarity lets you read the feedback.
2. Set an honest test budget. A week of signal, not a month of
   spend. For most beginner solopreneurs that is under a single
   week's meaningful marketing budget.
3. Run one creative — the same one-sentence promise you used on
   your flyers, rendered for that channel. Do not A/B test yet.
   A single message run honestly is faster to learn from than two
   muddled ones.
4. At the end of the week, compare the introductions this preset
   produced against the warm and local presets. If paid did not
   beat local, stop the spend and return to the earlier presets.

## What to learn from it

Paid outreach is a measurement instrument first and a channel
second. The goal of the test week is not volume — it is to know
the cost of a single honest introduction on that channel. That
number, however inconvenient, is what lets you choose between
deeper paid investment, going back to local and warm, or changing
the offer itself.

## Honest limits

This preset does not promise reach. It promises a decision. A week
of careful spend will tell you whether paid belongs in your mix
at all — most solopreneurs at this stage find that it does not
yet, and that is a useful answer. The last honest move is to turn
the ads off on Friday regardless of result, write the number down,
and return to the warm preset before spending again.
