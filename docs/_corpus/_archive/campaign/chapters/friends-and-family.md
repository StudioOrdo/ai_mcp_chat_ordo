# Friends and family

The warmest, lowest-friction start is to tell five people you already
trust what you are building. This guide walks you through that first
conversation without turning it into a sales pitch.

## Why start here

Your warmest audience is the only audience where you do not have to
earn attention — you already have it. That is a rare thing and worth
using deliberately. Five honest introductions from five people who
know what you do is usually enough signal to see whether anything in
your current offer is worth pressing harder on.

The goal of this preset is not to close anyone. It is to make the
first shape of your funnel real, so later guides (local flyers,
lightweight paid outreach) have something to compare against.

## How to run it

1. Open the referrals workspace and copy your personal link.
2. Pick five people you would happily catch up with even if this
   project did not exist. Ideally, three who already know your work
   and two who do not.
3. Send each of them a plain, honest message — one or two sentences
   about what you are trying, and the link. Do not attach more.
4. Answer questions as they come in. The ones who ask questions are
   telling you which part of the offer is unclear.
5. Come back tomorrow. If nobody has replied, that itself is signal —
   write down what you would change and move on to the next preset.

## Honest limits

This preset will not scale. It is a calibration tool, not a growth
engine. If it produces more than two genuine introductions in a week,
you can stop here for a while and focus on delivering what you
already have. If it produces zero, the local-flyers preset is a
better next step — do not escalate straight to paid outreach.
