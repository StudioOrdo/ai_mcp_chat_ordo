# Premium audience signals

This note is intentionally reserved for premium-tier search validation.
It gives one deeper pattern to look for once a founder already has a
steady stream of basic conversations.

## The margin recovery loop

Look for the moment when a prospect repeats the same operational pain in
slightly different words two or three times. That repetition usually
means the problem is costly enough to justify a clearer offer, a tighter
scope, or a faster delivery promise.

## How to use it

Do not treat this as a new campaign. Treat it as a search lens for
reviewing the notes you already have. If the same pain appears across
multiple conversations, that is the signal to rewrite the offer before
you buy more attention.
