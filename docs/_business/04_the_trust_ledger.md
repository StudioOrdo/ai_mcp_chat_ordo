## Why Trust Infrastructure Matters

As generated content volume rises, trust and attribution become the scarce
distribution channels.

Ordo treats trust as operational data without reducing people to transactions.

The aim is economic sovereignty for small operators: preserve who introduced
whom, who helped whom close work, and how value should flow back through the
network.

## Analog To Digital Loop

The QR and referral flow remains the first distribution wedge:

1. physical interaction or referral
2. attributed entry into persistent conversation
3. tracked progression through lead, consult, deal, delivery, follow-up

The human creates trust. The system preserves continuity and accounting.

## Referral Ledger Contract

Every attributed relationship event should be:

- traceable
- reviewable
- compensable where policy allows
- privacy-safe by default

## Network Effect

The long-term effect is an operator network where trust can compound without
becoming opaque.

This is not growth hacking. It is relationship continuity with accountable
infrastructure.

As implementation costs fall, this trust ledger becomes more important than
tool novelty. The operators who are most helpful, reliable, and high-taste gain
compounding advantage.
