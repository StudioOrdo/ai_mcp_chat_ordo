## Economic Inversion

Implementation is becoming abundant. Judgment remains scarce.

In this environment, copycats are normal. Product mechanics diffuse quickly.
The surviving advantage is the network around the product: trusted operators,
review discipline, and outcome reputation.

As model quality increases, the value shifts from producing artifacts to deciding:

- what should be produced
- what evidence is acceptable
- what quality bar is non-negotiable
- what next action is correct

## What Customers Actually Buy

Operators do not buy tools. They buy outcomes with continuity.

They want:

- leads followed up
- content shipped
- client commitments tracked
- quality enforced
- business context remembered over time

Ordo captures this demand by making governed execution and relationship
continuity the core product behavior.

## Solopreneur Cost Advantage

A solo operator with Ordo can reach team-level throughput without team-level
overhead.

This is the margin engine:

- low base infrastructure cost
- optional BYOK model spend
- metered charges only for expensive server paths

Lower implementation cost also unlocks aggressive distribution economics:

- higher affiliate commissions for qualified introductions
- rewards tied to retention and delivered outcomes, not raw signup volume
- sustainable payouts because production overhead stays low

## The Durable Moat

The moat is not one model provider.

The moat is the operating system for judgment plus execution:

- memory
- policy
- capability routing
- evidence and QA discipline
- workflow continuity

In short: the moat is coordinated trust and execution quality at network scale.
