# Stage 12 - Business Process Views

## Goal

Make the QR/referral/sales funnel visible as a workflow.

## Build

- Map QR scan to referral event.
- Map referral event to attributed conversation.
- Map conversation to lead.
- Map lead to consultation request.
- Map consultation to deal or training path.
- Add next-action and blocked-state projection.
- Add funnel timeline and queue views.

## Done

- Operators can see where trust turns into business outcome.
- The process is controllable without manually inspecting separate admin pages.

## Guardrails

- Do not model operations as content nodes.
- Do not create booking/commitment state until the current consult/deal process
  is mapped.
