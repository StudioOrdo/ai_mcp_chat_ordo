# Stage 11 - Ordo Development Work Order Loop

## Goal

Turn accepted reports into governed development work.

## Build

- Convert triaged reports into decision records.
- Convert decisions into specs or phase docs.
- Create work orders for implementation.
- Attach QA gates and verification requirements.
- Produce release notes and reporter follow-up.
- Capture lessons into future stages.

## Done

- Ordo development follows the same workflow doctrine as `ordo_process.md`.
- Public and private development status can be projected from durable state.

## Guardrails

- Do not allow automatic merge without human functional review.
- Do not let donation signals bypass QA or architecture authority.
