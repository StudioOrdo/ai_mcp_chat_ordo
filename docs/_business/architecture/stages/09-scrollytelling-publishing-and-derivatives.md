# Stage 09 - Scrollytelling Publishing And Derivatives

## Goal

Turn scrollytelling drafts into published releases and derivative assets.

## Build

- Define release artifact for scrollytelling pages.
- Add publish approval gate.
- Generate audio narration.
- Generate social shorts or short-form scripts.
- Generate social post copy.
- Track derivative lineage to the release.
- Add observe/revise loop for later improvements.

## Done

- A scrollytelling page can be published, revised, and traced.
- Audio and short-form derivatives are connected to the release.

## Guardrails

- Do not publish without QA state.
- Do not treat derivatives as disconnected files.
